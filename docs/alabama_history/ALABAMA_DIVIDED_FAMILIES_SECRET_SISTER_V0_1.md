## Secret Sister development

Secret Sister becomes the human-readable story grammar for crossing a divided-state scenario safely:

```text
SISTER OBSERVE <SCENE>
SISTER ASK <CLAIM>
SISTER CHECK <SOURCE>
SISTER HOLD <UNPROVEN_EDGE>
SISTER PLAY <GAME_STATE>
SISTER REPLAY <RECEIPT>
```

The syntax may route a story or verification request. It may not manufacture family relationships, historical facts, military orders, or authority.
