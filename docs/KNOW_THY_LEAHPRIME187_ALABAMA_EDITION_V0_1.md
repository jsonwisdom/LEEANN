## Core questions

Before a conclusion advances, ask:

1. What year and legal regime are we talking about?
2. Is the actor military, state civil, federal civil, or fictional?
3. What jurisdiction actually applies?
4. Is the source historical evidence, current law, public personnel information, or story material?
5. Did a real public record support the person-specific claim?
6. Did the story add a rank, command, authority, relationship, or legal effect that the source did not?
7. Can another reader replay the same evidence path?
