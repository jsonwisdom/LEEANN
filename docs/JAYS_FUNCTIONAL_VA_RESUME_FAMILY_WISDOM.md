## What Shall Unfold in a Family Environment?

A family member brings a memory, question, project, disagreement, or mission.

**BoxDee preserves first.** The original photo, message, story, document, receipt, or question is kept before anyone rewrites its meaning.

**Gray Baby minds the gap.** Missing information stays missing. A confident guess does not silently become family history.

**LeahPrime Comms translates.** Technical findings and contradictions are turned into plain family language.

**Boss Bre asks.** The child role may question, explore, compare, and learn. It does not become a combatant, investigator, surveillance node, or authority node.

**Replay returns everyone to the record.** If memories disagree, the family can inspect what was actually preserved and what remains unknown.

Work should unfold as **bounded missions**, not permanent roles. Each mission has a visible purpose, beginning, stopping point, and human owner.

Written receipts carry state so the entire system does not depend on one person remembering everything. A paused mission can restart from preserved state. Disagreement routes back to evidence and gaps instead of family rank.

Participation stays opt-in. Children explore; adults remain responsible for adult decisions.
