## Share

```text
SISTER SHARE RECEIPT <id>
```

Share produces a **share candidate**. Human review remains a boundary.

```text
SHARE != ENDORSEMENT
SHARE != AUTHORITY TRANSFER
SHARE != SOURCE PROMOTION
```

## Repeat

```text
SISTER REPEAT RECEIPT <id>
```

Repeat follows the same recorded source references, gaps, transforms, and route.

It does not silently reclassify a claim as verified merely because the replay ran twice.

```text
REPEAT != REVERIFY
```
